## 1. Introduction

Welcome to Trip Planner ("the App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Personal Data
The App does **not** require user registration or login. We do not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number, or account credentials.

### 2.2 Location Data
The App collects your device's **approximate and precise location** (GPS) to:
- Provide Google Maps-based location search and autocomplete
- Display relevant places near your trip destination
- Help you plan activities at specific locations

Location data is processed through **Google Maps Platform APIs** and is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

### 2.3 Photos and Media
The App may access your device's **camera and storage** to:
- Allow you to pick or capture photos for trip activities
- Display and manage photo galleries within your itineraries

Photos are stored **locally on your device** and are **not** uploaded to any external server.

### 2.4 Trip and Itinerary Data
The App stores your trip plans, itineraries, activity details, and notes **locally on your device** using SQLite database. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly share or export it.

### 2.5 AI-Generated Content
When you use the AI itinerary suggestion feature, your trip preferences (destination, dates, budget, interests) are sent to **OpenAI's GPT-4o API** to generate itinerary suggestions. This data is processed by OpenAI and is subject to [OpenAI's Privacy Policy](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy). OpenAI does not use this data to train models.

### 2.6 Device and Usage Data
The App does **not** collect device identifiers, usage analytics, crash reports, advertising data, or any other device-level information. There are **no analytics SDKs, ad networks, or tracking tools** integrated in this App.

## 5. Data Storage and Security

- All trip data, itineraries, and photos are stored **locally on your device** using SQLite and the device file system.
- **No data is transmitted to or stored on our servers** — the App operates entirely offline except for Google Maps and OpenAI API calls.
- We do not employ additional encryption beyond what your device operating system provides by default.
- You may delete all your data at any time by uninstalling the App or using the delete functions within the App.

---

## 6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do **not** sell, trade, or rent your information to third parties. Your data may only be shared in the following circumstances:

- **Explicit User Action:** When you use the "Share" feature to export or share your itineraries/photos with others.
- **PDF Export:** When you generate and export a PDF of your trip plan, the file is saved locally and shared at your discretion.
- **Legal Requirements:** We may disclose your data if required by law or in response to valid legal processes.
